# RoofHQ — Roofing Estimating & Proposals for Solo Roofers

RoofHQ (https://tryroofhq.com) is roofing software built by a selling roofer for the
one-truck shop. No per-report fees, no sales calls required — a tool that prices like a
contractor, not a subscription mill.

## What it does
- **DIY aerial measure reports** — full satellite measurement, no per-report fees
- **Drag-a-laser estimating** — line-item labor & material takeoff in minutes
- **Client proposals in one click** — professional PDF + portal delivery
- **Free tier for solo roofers** — flat-fee pricing, no hidden report charges

## Why it exists
The roofing-software market targets 3–30 man crews with per-report pricing that quietly
doubles a solo contractor's software bill. RoofHQ is the alternative: pay one flat fee,
measure your own roofs, keep your margin.

## Stack
Single-page app, end-to-end quote builder, PDF/portal delivery. Built by roofers.
